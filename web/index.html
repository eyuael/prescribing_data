Prescriptions per 1,000 registered patients, by deprivation decile

Monthly NHS prescription items dispensed in the community in England, divided by each practice's registered patients. Decile 1 is the most deprived tenth of neighbourhoods, decile 10 the least.

Items are summed across all practices in each decile, then divided by the summed list sizes, so large practices count in proportion to their patients. Practices without a list size or a postcode match are left out of both sides of the ratio. Deprivation is the English Indices of Deprivation 2025, matched through the practice postcode. The vertical axis starts at zero.

Sources: NHS Business Services Authority (English Prescribing Dataset), NHS England (Patients Registered at a GP Practice), ONS (Postcode Directory), MHCLG (English Indices of Deprivation 2025).
